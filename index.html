Dhamma Prasada

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 October 2026

Dhamma Prasada is a free mobile application that provides access to Sri Lankan Pirith audio and related Dhamma content.

This Privacy Policy explains how Dhamma Prasada handles user information.

Information We Collect

Dhamma Prasada does not collect, store, or share personal information from users.

The app does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

Dhamma Prasada does not request access to:

  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Photos or media
  • SMS or call information
  • Device files for personal data collection

How Information Is Used

Because Dhamma Prasada does not collect personal information, we do not use personal information for advertising, analytics, tracking, profiling, or other purposes.

The Pirith audio included in the app is provided as part of the app's functionality and is stored within the application.

Information Sharing

Dhamma Prasada does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties.

The app does not contain advertising or third-party analytics services that collect personal information.

Data Security

Since Dhamma Prasada does not collect or store personal information, there is no personal user data maintained by us that requires deletion or account management.

Data Retention and Deletion

Dhamma Prasada does not collect or maintain personal user data.

There are no user accounts and therefore no account data to delete.

Children's Privacy

Dhamma Prasada does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users. The app does not require users to provide personal information to use its features.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updated version will be made available on the same privacy policy webpage.
